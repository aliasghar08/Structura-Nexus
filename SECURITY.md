## 3. Reporting Copyright Infringement & Unauthorized Clones

Structura Nexus is proprietary software created and owned by **Ali Asghar** (`aliasgharinnocent@yahoo.com`). 

If you encounter an unauthorized clone, mirror, re-uploaded package, or deployment claiming to be Structura Nexus or using its source code without author attribution and copyright compliance:

- **Send Infringement Reports to:** [aliasgharinnocent@yahoo.com](mailto:aliasgharinnocent@yahoo.com)
- **Subject:** `[DMCA Infringement Report] Structura Nexus Unauthorized Use`
- **Details Required:**
  1. Infringing URL / Repository / Store Link.
  2. Description of infringing material (code, UI, trained neural weights, or branding).
  3. Identity of the infringing party (if known).

All validated infringements are immediately processed under the **Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512(c))** and result in formal DMCA takedown orders and copyright strikes.
